## [Security Expert Urges Developers to Stop Using Passkeys for Data Encryption](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/27/passkeys/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Security expert Tim Cappalli issued a public plea for the identity industry to stop promoting and using passkeys to encrypt user data. This warning highlights the critical risk of permanent, unrecoverable data loss when users inevitably lose their passkeys. This matters because misusing passkeys for encryption creates a severe usability and data recovery problem that undermines their primary benefit as phishing-resistant authentication credentials. If developers follow this flawed pattern at scale, it could lead to widespread data loss incidents and erode user trust in passkey technology. The warning specifically addresses the misuse of the WebAuthn PRF (Pseudo-Random Function) extension, which can generate encryption keys from passkeys. A key caveat is that passkeys, unlike traditional passwords, often lack user-recoverable backup mechanisms, making encrypted data permanently inaccessible if the single passkey is lost.

rss · Simon Willison · Feb 27, 22:49

**Background**: Passkeys are a modern authentication standard based on public key cryptography, designed to replace passwords. They are typically system-generated cryptographic key pairs (public and private) stored on a user's device or synced via a cloud service. While excellent for secure, phishing-resistant login, they are not designed as a general-purpose key derivation mechanism for data encryption, which requires robust backup and recovery strategies like key escrow.

This incident highlights a critical security failure by a government agency handling seized digital assets, undermining public trust in institutional cryptocurrency management. The exposed seed phrase belonged to a Ledger hardware wallet, and the image was published without any redaction. On-chain data revealed that at least three wallets, which had been inactive since January 2023 and collectively held 40% of PRTG's total supply, were affected. PRTG is a low-liquidity token traded primarily on MEXC, where a sell order of just $59 could cause a 2% price drop.

telegram · zaihuapd · Feb 28, 11:27

**Background**: A seed phrase, also known as a secret recovery phrase, is a series of words (typically 12 or 24) that grants complete control over a cryptocurrency wallet and all assets within it. Hardware wallets like Ledger are physical devices designed to store these seed phrases offline for enhanced security, making the seed phrase the single most critical piece of information to protect. If a seed phrase is exposed, anyone with access to it can transfer the assets without needing the physical hardware wallet itself.
