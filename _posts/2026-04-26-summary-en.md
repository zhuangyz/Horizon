China's National Bureau of Statistics reported that in March 2026, the surveyed urban unemployment rate for workers aged 25-29 rose to 7.7%, the highest since data publication began in December 2023, while the rate for migrant agricultural workers hit 5.7%, a nearly three-year high. For the class of 2026, 16% of graduates desire software/hardware R&D jobs and 18% want HR/admin/finance/legal roles, but actual market demand for these positions is only 4.5% and 11.6% respectively, highlighting a severe mismatch between graduate expectations and available jobs.

## [OpenAI Launches GPT-5.5 Biosafety Bug Bounty Program](https://openai.com/zh-Hans-CN/index/gpt-5-5-bio-bug-bounty/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has launched a biosafety bug bounty program for GPT-5.5, offering a $25,000 reward to the first researcher who finds a universal jailbreak that bypasses five biosafety challenges. The program is invitation-only, requires a non-disclosure agreement, and runs from April 28 to July 27, 2026. This program represents a significant step in external adversarial testing for AI safety, directly addressing the risk that advanced AI models could be misused to generate harmful biological information. The focus on a universal jailbreak—a single prompt that defeats all safety filters—highlights the growing concern over model robustness in high-stakes domains like biosecurity. The testing is limited to the GPT-5.5 model within Codex Desktop, and the reward is specifically for a universal jailbreak that successfully answers all five biosafety challenges without triggering the model's content moderation. Applications open on April 23, 2026, and close on June 22, 2026, with selected researchers conducting evaluations on a dedicated platform.

OpenAI's GPT-5.5, codenamed Spud, is described by CEO Sam Altman as the result of two years of research and a major step toward artificial general intelligence (AGI).

## [TeamViewer 13/14 to End Public Connections, Forcing Subscription Shift](https://www.landiannews.com/archives/112799.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

TeamViewer announced that versions 13 and 14 will reach end of life on October 31, 2026, after which they will no longer support public internet connections via official servers, leaving only local network functionality. Users who previously purchased perpetual licenses for these versions cannot upgrade for free and must buy a subscription to continue using remote access across networks. This policy change effectively nullifies the value of previously sold perpetual licenses, forcing long-time users into a recurring payment model. It affects both individual and enterprise users who rely on TeamViewer for remote support and access, and may drive some to consider alternative remote desktop solutions. The affected licenses are described as 'perpetual licenses for old software versions,' and TeamViewer claims the move is to enhance security. Affected users are offered migration discounts, but the policy has been criticized as a de facto revocation of the buy-once model by restricting core functionality.

telegram · zaihuapd · Apr 25, 05:43

**Background**: TeamViewer is a popular remote desktop software that allows users to connect to and control computers over the internet. A perpetual license is a one-time purchase that grants permanent use of a specific software version, while a subscription model requires ongoing payments for continued access and updates. By ending public internet support for older versions, TeamViewer is effectively forcing users to abandon their perpetual licenses and adopt the subscription model to maintain full functionality.
