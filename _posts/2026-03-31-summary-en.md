## [Micron Bets on Stacked GDDR, Targets 2027 for First Samples](https://www.etnews.com/20260330000228) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Micron has initiated R&D on stacked GDDR memory, with plans to complete equipment deployment and begin process testing in the second half of 2026, aiming to release samples of approximately 4-layer stacked GDDR as early as 2027. This new product is positioned between HBM and standard GDDR, targeting AI accelerators and other applications by offering higher bandwidth than standard GDDR but at a lower cost than HBM. This development is significant because it could create a new, cost-effective high-bandwidth memory tier for the booming AI hardware market, potentially offering an alternative to expensive HBM for applications like AI inference and high-performance gaming GPUs. If Micron commercializes this technology first, it could gain a competitive edge in emerging market segments where competitors Samsung and SK Hynix have not yet announced similar plans. The technology currently has no precedent for mass production, and Micron faces challenges including chip interconnect, power consumption, thermal management, and cost control due to the stacking process. The report notes that Samsung and SK Hynix have not publicly disclosed similar plans, potentially giving Micron a first-mover advantage.

telegram · zaihuapd · Mar 31, 00:36

**Background**: GDDR (Graphics Double Data Rate) memory is a type of high-speed DRAM traditionally used in graphics cards, offering high bandwidth but typically in a 2D planar layout. HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) is a more advanced, vertically stacked memory technology that achieves even higher bandwidth and density by stacking multiple DRAM dies and connecting them with Through-Silicon Vias (TSVs), but it comes at a significantly higher cost and complexity. Stacked GDDR appears to be an attempt to bridge the gap, applying some 3D stacking principles to GDDR architecture to improve performance while aiming to keep costs lower than full HBM implementations.
