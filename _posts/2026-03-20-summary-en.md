## [Chinese Scientists Uncover Genetic Secret of Perennial Rice, Enabling "Rejuvenation"](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=13573676469936057762) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A team led by Academician Han Bin and Wang Jiawei from the Chinese Academy of Sciences published a cover paper in Science, identifying the EBT1 gene locus as the core genetic mechanism that allows wild rice to revert from reproductive growth back to vegetative growth after flowering. By introducing EBT1 along with PROG1 and TIG1 genes, they successfully created a "wild rice-like" variety that can survive and be harvested for at least two years in the field. This discovery fundamentally challenges the traditional understanding of rice as an annual crop and provides a new genetic resource and theoretical foundation for developing low-carbon, sustainable perennial grain crops. It has significant implications for global food security and sustainable agricultural practices by potentially reducing the need for annual replanting, saving labor, and minimizing soil disturbance. The EBT1 locus is composed of two tandem microRNA genes, MIR156B and MIR156C, which are responsible for floral reversion and vegetative propagation. The perennial trait involves an epigenetic "reset" in tiller buds, characterized by a reduction in the repressive histone modification H3K27me3 and increased chromatin openness, which reactivates MIR156 expression.

telegram · zaihuapd · Mar 20, 12:55

**Background**: Most cultivated rice is annual, meaning it completes its life cycle (from seed to seed) within one year and then dies. In contrast, some wild rice species are perennial, capable of surviving and regrowing for multiple years after an initial planting. During the domestication of rice over millennia, humans selectively bred for annual traits (like synchronized seed production) which inadvertently led to the loss of the perennial "rejuvenation" ability found in wild ancestors. MicroRNAs are small RNA molecules that regulate gene expression by targeting messenger RNAs.

The alleged scheme involved using Southeast Asian shell companies, falsified documents, and thousands of non-functional counterfeit servers to deceive audits and inspections. This case highlights the intense enforcement of U.S. export controls on cutting-edge AI technology and the high-stakes efforts by some entities to circumvent them. The alleged diversion of such a massive volume of restricted hardware, primarily Nvidia's H100 and H200 GPUs, could significantly impact the global AI development race and supply chain security. The defendants allegedly used a complex, two-year scheme that included placing fake servers in warehouses and using tools like hair dryers to remove and replace serial number labels to conceal the shipments. Super Micro, which accounts for about 9% of Nvidia's revenue, has suspended the accused employees and stated it maintains a robust compliance program.
