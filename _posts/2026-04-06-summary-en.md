## [Scientists engineer tobacco to produce five natural psychedelics with up to 40x yield boost](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aeb3034) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers from the Weizmann Institute of Science and other institutions have genetically engineered Nicotiana benthamiana (tobacco) plants to biosynthesize five natural psychedelic tryptamines, including DMT, psilocybin, and 5-MeO-DMT. Using AlphaFold3-guided protein engineering, they achieved a 40-fold increase in the yield of 5-MeO-DMT and created a platform capable of producing non-natural halogenated derivatives. This work establishes a sustainable, scalable, and potentially 'cruelty-free' production platform for psychedelic compounds, which are being investigated as novel therapeutics for mental health conditions like depression, anxiety, and PTSD. It addresses ecological concerns from traditional extraction methods (e.g., overharvesting fungi or toad secretions) and demonstrates the power of modern computational biology tools like AlphaFold3 in synthetic biology applications. The system repurposes the plant's endogenous tryptophan as a starting material to reconstruct biosynthetic pathways from plants, fungi, and animals within a single host. A key breakthrough was using AlphaFold3 to predict protein structures and guide directed mutations of enzymes, specifically an N-methyltransferase, to dramatically improve catalytic efficiency and yield for 5-MeO-DMT.

## [Chinese researchers develop self-protecting electrolyte to prevent thermal runaway in Ah-level sodium-ion batteries](https://api3.cls.cn/share/article/2335878?os=android&amp;sv=8.7.5&amp;app=cailianpress) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A team from the Chinese Academy of Sciences has developed a polymerizable non-flammable electrolyte (PNE) that automatically solidifies into a dense barrier at temperatures above 150°C, completely blocking thermal runaway in Ah-level sodium-ion batteries. This breakthrough, published in Nature Energy, represents the first time thermal runaway has been completely prevented in such large-capacity sodium-ion cells. This development addresses a critical safety challenge that has hindered the commercialization of sodium-ion batteries for electric vehicles and large-scale energy storage. By providing a smart, multi-layered safety system that doesn't sacrifice performance, it could accelerate the adoption of more affordable and sustainable battery alternatives to lithium-ion technology. The PNE creates a "smart firewall" inside the battery that physically isolates thermal runaway pathways when triggered by high temperatures. Importantly, this safety breakthrough maintains excellent wide-temperature performance and high-voltage stability, meaning it doesn't compromise the battery's operational capabilities.

**Background**: Sodium-ion batteries are emerging as a promising alternative to lithium-ion batteries because sodium is more abundant and cheaper than lithium. Thermal runaway is an uncontrollable chain reaction in batteries that generates intense heat, toxic smoke, and can lead to fires or explosions, representing a major safety concern especially for large-capacity (Ah-level) cells used in EVs and grid storage. Traditional approaches focused primarily on flame-retardant electrolytes, but this new research demonstrates that safety requires a more comprehensive "thermal stability-interface stability-physical isolation" system.

## [SGLang v0.5.10 released with major performance optimizations for LLM serving.](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/releases/tag/v0.5.10) ⭐️ 7.0/10

SGLang v0.5.10 introduces piecewise CUDA graph execution as the default, integrates Elastic EP for partial failure tolerance in MoE models, adds GPU staging buffers to boost RDMA efficiency, and incorporates HiSparse for sparse attention. It also includes support for FlashInfer MXFP8 kernels, upgrades to Transformers 5.3.0, and adds LoRA support for MoE layers. These optimizations directly address critical production challenges in large-scale LLM inference, such as improving throughput for complex models, ensuring service continuity during hardware failures, and reducing communication overhead in distributed systems. This release enhances the efficiency and reliability of serving state-of-the-art models like DeepSeek MoE, Qwen, and GLM-5 in production environments. The GPU staging buffer for PD disaggregation reduces RDMA request counts by ~1000x for GQA models, leading to a ~5x increase in TPS/GPU under high concurrency. The Elastic EP integration specifically targets DeepSeek MoE deployments, allowing the system to redistribute expert weights and continue serving without a full restart when a GPU fails.

github · Fridge003 · Apr 6, 04:42

**Background**: SGLang is a high-performance language for programming and serving large language models (LLMs). CUDA graphs are a technique to reduce kernel launch overhead in GPU computing by capturing a sequence of operations and replaying them; piecewise CUDA graphs split the model into segments to handle dynamic control flow better. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, like DeepSeek-V3, use a sparse architecture where different parts of the model (experts) are activated per input, posing challenges for efficient and fault-tolerant distributed serving. RDMA (Remote Direct Memory Access) allows direct memory access between machines, bypassing the CPU, which is crucial for low-latency distributed inference.

## [Developer builds comprehensive SQLite linting tools in three months using AI after eight-year delay](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/building-with-ai/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Developer Lalit Maganti used Claude Code to build syntaqlite—a parser, formatter, and verifier for SQLite—in just three months, after conceptualizing the project for eight years. The AI helped overcome the initial hurdle of implementing over 400 grammar rules, but Maganti ultimately discarded the first AI-generated prototype and rebuilt the library with more deliberate human architectural design. This demonstrates a practical, nuanced application of agentic engineering where AI accelerates implementation of tedious tasks but human expertise remains critical for high-level design. It provides the SQLite ecosystem with high-fidelity, production-ready developer tools that previously relied on generic or incomplete solutions, potentially improving development workflows and code quality for countless projects. Syntaqlite is built directly on SQLite's own grammar and tokenizer, ensuring it matches SQLite's exact syntax and behavior, including version-specific features and compile-time flags. The project is currently at version 0.x, meaning its APIs and CLI flags may change before a stable 1.0 release.
