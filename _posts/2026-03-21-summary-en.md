## [Chinese Scientists Develop Longevity Rice by Unlocking Genetic Secrets of Perennial Growth](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=13573676469936057762) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A team led by Academician Han Bin and Wang Jiawei from the Chinese Academy of Sciences published a cover paper in Science, identifying the EBT1 gene locus—composed of tandem microRNA genes MIR156BC—as the core genetic switch enabling rice to revert from reproductive growth back to vegetative growth after flowering. By introducing EBT1 along with PROG1 and TIG1 genes, they successfully created a 'wild rice-like' variety that can survive for at least two years in the field, achieving 'single planting, continuous harvesting.' This discovery fundamentally challenges the traditional understanding of rice as an annual crop and provides a novel genetic resource and theoretical foundation for developing low-carbon, sustainable perennial grain crops. It has significant implications for global food security by potentially reducing the labor, water, and soil disturbance associated with annual replanting, contributing to more sustainable agricultural practices. The perennial trait involves an epigenetic 'reset' mechanism where wild rice reduces the repressive histone modification H3K27me3 and increases chromatin openness in tiller buds, reactivating MIR156 expression to revert the plant to a juvenile state. The engineered rice variety combines the key genetic elements for both perennial growth (EBT1) and prostrate growth habit (PROG1, TIG1), mimicking the wild ancestor's lifecycle.

telegram · zaihuapd · Mar 20, 12:55

**Background**: Most cultivated rice is annual, meaning it completes its lifecycle (from germination to seed production and death) within one year or season, requiring replanting each cycle. In contrast, perennial plants can live for multiple years, regrowing from the same root system. MicroRNAs like miR156 are small non-coding RNAs that regulate gene expression and are known to play roles in plant development, including the transition from juvenile to adult phases and flowering time. Histone modifications, such as H3K27me3, are chemical changes to the proteins that package DNA, influencing how tightly DNA is wound and thus whether genes are accessible for expression or silenced.

## [vLLM v0.18.0 released with gRPC serving, GPU-less rendering, and GPU-based NGram speculative decoding.](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/releases/tag/v0.18.0) ⭐️ 7.0/10

vLLM v0.18.0, a major release with 445 commits from 213 contributors, introduces gRPC serving support via a new `--grpc` flag, a GPU-less render serving mode for multimodal preprocessing, and a GPU-accelerated implementation of NGram speculative decoding. It also includes significant KV cache offloading improvements, Elastic Expert Parallelism milestone 2, and support for new model architectures like Sarvam MoE and OLMo Hybrid. This release significantly enhances vLLM's versatility and performance for production LLM serving. The addition of gRPC provides a high-performance RPC alternative to HTTP, GPU-less rendering enables cost-effective scaling of multimodal workloads, and GPU-based NGram speculative decoding reduces latency for structured outputs, collectively making vLLM more suitable for diverse, large-scale deployment scenarios. The release notes a known issue of degraded accuracy when serving Qwen3.5 with FP8 KV cache on NVIDIA B200 GPUs. Notably, Ray is no longer a default dependency and must be installed explicitly if needed. The update also includes FlashInfer 0.6.6 for performance gains and introduces a new `--enable-ep-weight-filter` CLI option for faster loading of Elastic Parallelism models.

github · khluu · Mar 20, 21:31

**Background**: vLLM is a high-throughput and memory-efficient inference and serving engine for Large Language Models (LLMs). Speculative decoding is a technique to speed up LLM inference by using a faster, less accurate 'draft' model to propose tokens, which are then verified by the main model; NGram speculative decoding uses known token patterns (N-grams) as the draft. KV cache offloading is a technique that moves attention key/value data from GPU memory to lower-cost storage like CPU memory to free up GPU resources while preserving the ability to resume inference.
